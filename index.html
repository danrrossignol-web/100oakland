Latest Project Updates

Unanimous Inland Wetlands Approval (June 2026)

Our team is proud to announce that our application has received unanimous approval from the South Windsor Inland Wetlands Agency and Conservation Commission. We greatly appreciate the collaborative discussions with the town. Our finalized site design permanently preserves 12 acres of natural open space, removes invasive species like Japanese Knotweed, and features a custom-engineered 60-foot bridge to safeguard the local brook and allow wildlife to move freely.

Next Steps: We are currently preparing for our presentation to the South Windsor Planning & Zoning Commission in October.

Keeping Our Neighbors Close to Home

As our community evolves, many long-time residents find themselves looking to downsize from larger family homes, but struggle to find high-quality, low-maintenance options locally. Too often, downsizing means having to leave the community, friends, and local businesses they have supported for decades.

The Estates at 100 Oakland is designed specifically to fill that gap. By providing a quiet, carefully planned 55+ neighborhood, we are offering our neighbors the opportunity to transition into a single-story home with ease—ensuring they can keep their roots in South Windsor.

Benefits to South Windsor

🏡

Single-Story Living

Offering accessible, high-quality home designs tailored for active adults looking to downsize without compromising on craftsmanship or community.

🛣️

Privately Maintained

All neighborhood roads will be privately owned and maintained by the HOA. Snow plowing, paving, and street lighting will be handled entirely internally, keeping ongoing infrastructure costs off the town's road maintenance budget.

📈

Positive Municipal Impact

Designed to generate strong, consistent property tax revenue for South Windsor to support local services, while naturally avoiding the impacts on school enrollment associated with standard residential development.

Building Connection Right at Home

Active adult living is about more than just a beautiful home—it’s about shared experiences. Our plan includes a dedicated, private community clubhouse designed specifically for residents to gather, host events, and stay active. Featuring versatile social spaces and a welcoming gathering hall, this on-site amenity provides a private hub for everyday neighborly connection, perfectly complementing the wonderful community resources South Windsor already offers.
